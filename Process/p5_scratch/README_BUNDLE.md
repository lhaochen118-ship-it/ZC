## Key resource numbers

- **Baseline:** `configs/flow_p5_zs_strict.json`, strict zero-shot **0.754 mean** on two D1 seeds; fine-tuned P5 0.952 is a *reference only*.
- **G1 priority screen:** 2 Kaggle accounts × 2 concurrent L4 sessions/account, 4 L4/session = **16 GPUs concurrent**; **9.4–16.4 GPU-hours** (planning estimate).
- **All 14 variants at screening if every conditional gate passes:** **35.6–65.0 GPU-hours**, **22.25–40.63 Kaggle quota-hours** estimated with historical 2.5× factor.
- **Full D1 final assessment:** 1–3 finalists × 2 seeds × 4 L4/session. Up to **3 accounts / 6 concurrent GPU sessions / 24 L4** for three finalists. For ordinary two finalists: **30.4–56 GPU-hours** more.
- **FAST scenario total:** **39.8–72.4 GPU-hours** before extra preflight/debug, assuming four priority GPU screens and two finalists on two seeds.
- **Account usage:** A01–A19 are anonymous capacity roles; historical guide mentions 19 accounts, but current availability and quotas **not verified**. Plan is to pack two sessions per *used* account; do not launch extra accounts merely because they exist.

## Grounding and limitations

Actual account GPU quota, user credentials, available snapshots, N1–N7 Kaggle results and model runtime are **not verified** in this authoring environment. No Kaggle kernel for N1–N7 was launched while producing this package. Pilot estimates should be updated after profiling 30–50 cases per variant on the production code.

The `new7_resource_schedule.json` is a **planning artifact**, not a `automation/launch_many.py`-compatible plan. Its experiments must first be implemented and wrapped into repo-specific `automation/jobs/*.jobs` definitions and Kaggle plan entries, with live quota checks, immutable code/dataset assets and zero-shot business-rule compliance. Do not store Kaggle API tokens inside this ZIP or repository.
